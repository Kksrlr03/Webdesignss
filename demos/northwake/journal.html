The art of
doing a little less.

A guide to leaving space in your next escape.

A slower travel journal

Start with one thing.

Choose one thing you would love to do each day. A walk. A swim. A long breakfast with a view. Let everything else become a possibility rather than a requirement.

Pack for the ordinary moments.

The book you have been meaning to start. A layer that makes the evening comfortable. Something good to share at the table. It is often the small things that make a place feel like yours.

Pay attention to the transitions.

Morning becoming afternoon. A bright sky giving way to a softer one. Those in-between moments rarely make an itinerary, but they are often the ones you remember.

Travel does not always need to change your life. Sometimes it is enough to change the pace of it.
