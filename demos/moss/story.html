Our story

For the moments
in between.

Moss & Whisk is an imagined tea brand with a very real point of view: little rituals can make an ordinary day feel considered.

Born from
a slower morning.

We imagined a brand that takes the intimidation out of tea. Clear guidance, friendly language and objects that are lovely to use—not just lovely to look at.


The palette comes from the cup itself: soft cream, deep leaf green and that unmistakable bright moss. The experience is tactile, playful and deliberately unhurried.

What we make room for.

Simple values behind this fictional brand.

Everyday ease

Small choices and clear guidance that make beginning feel natural.

Tactile pleasure

A bowl that fits your hands. Packaging that feels like part of the ritual.

Your own pace

No perfect routine required. Just a moment you choose to keep.
