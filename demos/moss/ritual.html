The ritual

Less perfection.
More presence.

There is no secret handshake. Just a bowl, a whisk, and a little time.

Your first bowl.

A simple starting point. Adjust to your taste.

01

Sift a little

Start with about 2g of matcha. Sifting helps break up the little clumps before you add water.

02

Add warm water

Pour in a small amount of warm, not boiling, water. Begin with around 60ml and find the strength you enjoy.

03

Whisk & enjoy

Whisk in a quick W motion until lightly foamy. Enjoy as it is, or pour over your favourite milk and ice.

Make it your own

A ritual,
not a rulebook.

Some days it is a slow bowl by the window. Some days it is an iced latte on the way out. The point is finding a moment that feels like yours.
