The luxury
of less.

A short studio essay on daylight, honest materials and the spaces between.

Design journal / 5 minute read

Leave room for the unexpected.

Not every corner needs an object. Not every wall needs a feature. A pause in a room can be as useful as a pause in a conversation: it lets everything else come into focus.

We like spaces that offer a little freedom. A wide window ledge becomes a reading seat. A threshold becomes a place to sit with a cup of tea. A generous landing becomes a quiet workspace.

Let daylight do the decorating.

Before choosing a finish, watch how the light moves. The same plaster wall can feel cool at dawn and warm by late afternoon. Deep reveals and filtered openings make light tangible, without adding more things.

Materials that make sense.

Timber where your hand rests. Stone where the ground needs resilience. Soft surfaces where sound needs a little help. Choosing materials by how they are used gives a room its own honest character.
